ACEBRACE · BIOMEDICAL DEVICE

Engineering a smarter approach to knee support.

AceBrace is a wearable knee-support concept combining biomechanics, embedded electronics, real-time sensing, and software-based data tracking.

Biomedical Devices Biomechanics Wearable Technology Embedded Systems Product Development Patent
01 · THE PROBLEM

From a real clinical problem to an engineering concept.

AceBrace began with a practical question: how could a knee support provide more information about movement and loading instead of functioning only as a passive brace?

The project explored how wearable sensing could be integrated into a knee-support system to monitor movement and provide usable feedback.

02 · DESIGN DEVELOPMENT

Iterating from concept to physical prototype.

Multiple design iterations were developed to explore the physical architecture of the brace, component placement, and integration of sensing hardware.

Design 1

Initial wearable architecture and component layout.

Design 2

Refined structural concept developed through iterative design.

Prototype 1

Physical prototype demonstrating the transition from digital design to hardware.

03 · ELECTRONICS

Building the sensing hardware.

The project progressed beyond the physical brace into custom electronics and hardware integration. Components were assembled and soldered onto a custom PCB to support the sensing and data-collection system.

Custom PCB + Electronics

Custom circuit hardware and soldered electrical components used in the prototype system.

04 · REAL-TIME DATA

Turning movement into usable data.

AceBrace was designed to connect physical movement with digital measurement. The prototype demonstrates the collection and visualization of sensor data.

Data Demonstration 1

Demonstration of the prototype's real-time data collection system.

Custom App

Sensor data visualized through a custom software interface.

05 · ENGINEERING

Hardware, software, and biomechanics in one system.

Biomechanics

The brace was developed around the mechanics of knee movement and the goal of understanding how external support interacts with motion.

Embedded Hardware

Sensors and custom electronics were integrated into the physical prototype to capture movement data.

Data Tracking

Collected measurements were connected to a software interface for real-time visualization and tracking.

Product Development

The project progressed through multiple physical and digital iterations, moving from concept toward a functional prototype.

06 · PATENT & DEVELOPMENT

Developing the concept beyond the prototype.

AceBrace progressed from an initial invention concept toward formal intellectual-property development and continued hardware refinement.

The project has included patent development, prototype iteration, investor presentations, and exploration of manufacturing pathways.

07 · PRESENTATION

Communicating the engineering.

AceBrace has also been developed as a research and engineering project for presentation and external feedback.
